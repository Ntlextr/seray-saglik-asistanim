Privacy Policy — Seray Health Assistant

Last updated: August 2026

Seray Health Assistant ("the App") takes the security of its users' personal data seriously, in line with Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

1. Data Collected

The App processes your profile information (name, age, diabetes status, etc.) as well as the health data you enter, including your blood pressure, blood sugar, medication, nutrition, and e-Nabız lab result records.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

All of your data is stored only on your own device (in a local database). The App does not automatically send your data to any server or share it with third parties.

3. AI Analysis

When you choose to have your e-Nabız lab results, medications, or nutrition records analyzed, the relevant content is sent over a secure connection to the AI service provider used by the App, solely to generate that specific analysis. This content is not additionally stored by the App.

4. Device Permissions

The App requests camera access (for pulse measurement and photographing medicine boxes), notifications (for medication/water reminders), step counter/motion sensor access, and optional Health Connect access only when the related feature is used.

5. Backup and Sharing

When you use the "Backup" or "Share Results" features, the generated file is shared only with the app or person you choose. You are responsible for that sharing. If Android's built-in Auto Backup feature is enabled, your data may also be backed up at the operating system level through your Google account.

6. Your Rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your data is being processed, and to request its correction or deletion. Since your data lives on your device, uninstalling the App deletes all of your data as well.

7. Contact

For questions, you can contact Seray Teknoloji.
